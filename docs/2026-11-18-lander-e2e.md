# lander v001 hardware check (2026-11-18)

## Setup

- FreeWili 2 (`FREE-WILi2 FX0036`) on the on-board CMSIS-DAP probe, Windows.
- `fw build lander` + `fw ramrun lander`; driven with `fw press` / `fw hold`
  / `fw release` / `fw screenshot`, log from `fw rtt`.

## Observed results

- Boot log: `bmi323: chipid=0x1143 ok`, `lander: v001 up, imu=1 agentio=1`,
  required power zones `0x203`.
- Calibration screen rendered as drawn: STBY lamp, PROG 00, V16 N04, live
  accelerometer in R1-R3 (labels ACC X/Y/Z MG). Values changed between two
  sessions (4/4/-696 mg, later 79/838/-522 mg), so the IMU read is live.
- Two injected GREEN presses calibrated and started P64. With no physical
  tilt the right-tilt step fell back to board axis 1 (logged), as designed.
- P64 screen: PROG 64, V06 N60, altitude/rate/fuel registers updating, GUID
  panel showing guidance throttle and pitch commands (magenta).
- `fw hold green`: throttle bar and log rose to 99%; release returned to 10%.
- 1202 fired once, logged `PROGRAM ALARM 1202 at 1998 ft`. Captured during
  the restart window: V05 N09 shown, registers blank, guidance cue removed,
  PROG and RESTART lamps lit, message `1202 PROGRAM ALARM`.
- RED (RSET) cleared both lamps and returned to V06 N60 with guidance back.
- Crash path: `LM LOST AT 137.1 FPS`, end registers RANGE/HDOT/FUEL, AGAIN
  over the green button; AGAIN started a new descent.
- `fw test`: 73/73 including `lander_sim` and the app-contract suite.

## Not verified

- **Tilt steering with a real tilt.** Only the no-tilt fallback was
  exercised; the learned axis and its sign need a person holding the board.
- **A soft landing on hardware.** Shown only by the host test (guidance as
  autopilot, continuous and on/off throttle, ~0.3 m/s touchdown, 30% fuel).
- **HOME exit.** After an injected 8 s HOME hold the probe could no longer
  reach the app (agentio gone), consistent with a reboot into the stock
  firmware, but nobody looked at the panel to confirm.
- The V05 N09 phase after the restart window (R1/R3 `01202`) was not captured.
- LEDs were not observed (no camera).
